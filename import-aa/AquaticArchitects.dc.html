01The Brief

One studio. The full stack.

Aquatic Architects needed a digital home as considered as the marine spaces they design. Fast, immersive, and unmistakably theirs across every device.

We designed the site for desktop and mobile as one system: a deep-teal palette, editorial type, and a layout that lets each project breathe like an exhibit.

Interactive storytelling carries the visitor through every project: scenes reveal on scroll, sequences narrate the work, and the architecture is felt rather than listed.

Real-time 3D brings the structures into the browser. We optimised every scene so it loads fast and holds a smooth frame rate on phones as well as desktops.

AI-driven content and a social system keep the feed alive between launches: on palette, on brand, and produced in-studio.
