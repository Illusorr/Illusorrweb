What is Nanotern™

For the Farmer

NANOTERN is a soil water management technology designed to help farmers use water more efficiently. When applied to the soil, NANOTERN works like a smart water reservoir around the root zone. It absorbs and holds water, then gradually releases it back to the plant when the soil starts to dry and the plant needs moisture.

This helps the soil stay moist for longer, reduces water loss, and supports healthier root development. As a result, farmers can grow stronger plants while using less irrigation water.

NANOTERN helps your soil store water better, keeps moisture available near the roots of plants for longer, and supports better growth under drought or stressful conditions. It is biodegradable and non toxic, making it suitable for sustainable agriculture.

Technical Overview

For the Investor & Scientist

NANOTERN is a biodegradable agricultural nanotechnology designed to improve soil water management and increase resource efficiency in farming. Developed through long term scientific research, NANOTERN functions as a smart water retention system within the soil. It absorbs and stores significant amounts of water, then gradually releases moisture back to the root zone depending on soil conditions and plant demand.

From a scientific perspective, NANOTERN helps optimize the soil's water holding capacity, reduce water loss through drainage and evaporation, and maintain a more stable moisture environment around plant roots. This can support stronger root development, improved plant resilience under drought stress, and more efficient use of fertilizers and other agricultural inputs.

From an investment perspective, NANOTERN addresses one of the most critical global challenges in agriculture: producing more with less water. With increasing pressure on water resources, climate related drought risks, and rising input costs, technologies that improve water use efficiency offer strong scalability across water stressed agricultural markets.

What is Insease

For the Farmer

INSEASE is a smart greenhouse film designed to protect crops from insects for a longer period of time. Instead of applying insecticides repeatedly by spraying, INSEASE carries the active insect repelling ingredient inside the greenhouse cover film. This ingredient is released slowly and in controlled amounts, helping to keep harmful insects away from the crop.

INSEASE containing films release the active ingredient slowly in a rate that is just enough to repel insects. This way, the toxic compound does not touch the crop product or the soil, and the excessive use of insecticides is prevented without losing fertile soil.

For a farmer, this means: INSEASE helps protect greenhouse crops against pests such as aphids, while reducing the need for frequent pesticide spraying. It provides long lasting protection for up to 60 days, supports safer crop production, and helps reduce pesticide residues on plants and contamination of soil or water.

INSEASE turns the greenhouse cover itself into a protective layer against insects. The main point: PREVENTING EXCESSIVE USE OF INSECTICIDES.

[Insease Greenhouse Film Application Photography]
Technical Overview

For the Investor & Scientist

INSEASE is a patented greenhouse film technology designed to provide long term, sustained insect protection through nanotechnology based active ingredient release. Instead of relying only on repeated pesticide spraying, INSEASE integrates an insect repelling active ingredient into polymeric greenhouse cover films using naturally occurring nanotubes. These nanotubes act as carriers, enabling the active ingredient to be released slowly and in controlled amounts over time.

From a scientific perspective, INSEASE addresses a key limitation of conventional pesticide application: low targeting efficiency and unnecessary chemical exposure. By embedding the active ingredient directly into the greenhouse film, the technology helps create a protective environment around the crop while reducing direct pesticide residue on plants, soil, and surrounding ecosystems. The product is designed to provide sustained release and long lasting protection for up to 60 days, with effectiveness against pests.

From an investment perspective, INSEASE offers a scalable solution for controlled environment agriculture, particularly in greenhouse markets where pest pressure, residue concerns, labor costs, and repeated spraying cycles are major operational challenges.

INSEASE transforms greenhouse film from a passive cover material into an active crop protection system, combining polymer science, nanotechnology, and sustainable pest management.

What is Antimic Agro

For the Farmer

ANTIMIC AGRO is a water based antimicrobial protection product designed to help farmers keep crops, surfaces, and post harvest areas cleaner and safer. After harvest, microorganisms such as bacteria, fungi, viruses, and algae can reduce crop quality, cause spoilage, and shorten shelf life. ANTIMIC AGRO helps prevent these microorganisms from attaching to surfaces and multiplying.

When applied, it forms an invisible, odorless, non toxic and colorless nano sized protective shield on the surface. This shield supports long term hygiene and helps protect crop quality during production, storage, and the supply chain.

For a farmer, this means: ANTIMIC AGRO helps reduce microbial risks, keeps products fresher for longer, and supports cleaner greenhouse, storage, and handling environments. It can be used on a wide range of crop and facility surfaces, and it is described as water based, non toxic, free from heavy metals, and eco friendly when used correctly.

ANTIMIC AGRO acts like an invisible hygiene shield that helps protect crops from microbial spoilage and supports longer shelf life.

[Antimic Agro Application Photography]
Technical Overview

For the Investor & Scientist

ANTIMIC AGRO is a nanotechnology based antimicrobial protection system designed to preserve crop quality and extend shelf life across agricultural production, post harvest handling, and the supply chain. It addresses one of the major hidden risks in agriculture: microbial contamination. Bacteria, fungi, viruses, and algae can reduce crop quality, accelerate spoilage, shorten shelf life, and create hygiene risks during production, storage, transport, and post harvest processing.

Scientifically, ANTIMIC AGRO works by forming an invisible, odorless, and colorless nano sized protective shield on crop or surface areas. This shield helps prevent microorganisms from adhering to the surface, multiplying and cross contamination. It is positioned as a water based, non toxic, broad spectrum antimicrobial technology with antibacterial, antifungal, and antiviral properties.

From an investor perspective, ANTIMIC AGRO offers value by targeting crop loss, shelf life extension, food safety, and post harvest efficiency. It can be used across a wide range of agricultural surfaces and supply chain environments, including greenhouse surfaces, storage areas, irrigation related systems, and handling areas. The technology is also presented as long lasting, heavy metal free, and suitable for eco conscious agricultural practices.

ANTIMIC AGRO turns crop and facility surfaces into protected antimicrobial zones, helping reduce microbial spoilage, preserve product quality, and extend shelf life through advanced nanotechnology.
