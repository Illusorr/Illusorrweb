01The World

No hard architecture. Only membrane.

Pylon is an artistic 3D world built on procedural modeling and advanced design in Houdini FX, where every surface is a noise-displaced membrane.

The form is driven by algorithmic noise on the volume. Everything is a noise-displaced surface, the walls fold and ripple like molten metal frozen mid-flow.

That signature comes from layered Houdini noise, likely Perlin or Worley, pushed through a displacement and VDB workflow until the geometry reads as one continuous skin.

There is no hard architecture here, only a continuous organic membrane. It feels carved by fluid rather than built, a space that behaves more like a frozen current than a room.

Pylon was one of the first worlds we designed and launched, authored by artist Oleg Soroko as a statement of what design-led virtual space could be.

The World01

Virtual reality has given us the ability to create an experience that goes beyond spacetime, for the first time ever. It is, quite literally, a dream come true.

We are now able to exist, interact with others, and perform activities, in a completely simulated realm. We can have parties, host fashion shows, and conduct classes all in a space that is not physical.

To contribute to this, ILLUSORR is transitioning the design disciplines from the physical to the virtual world, creating the world's first design-oriented virtual platform.

Architects have always been the leaders of designing physical reality. Now it is our duty to make the transition into also designing the virtual realm.

For the first time, designers are leading the creation of a fully immersive experience where attendees engage with the latest frontiers of the design world.

02The Launch

A three-day event. December 11 · 12 · 13.

Pylon hosted the ILLUSORR launch event in 2020, a three-day gathering of artists, architects, musicians and the public inside a single virtual world.

Dec 11 01/03

Opening night. Talks and exhibitions launch the world, with the first concerts after dark.

Dec 12 02/03

Fashion shows, stores and workshops fill the membrane as the program reaches full scale.

Dec 13 03/03

Closing day. Headline performances and a final showcase of the design frontier.
