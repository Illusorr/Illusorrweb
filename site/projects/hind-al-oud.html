01 The Brief

A new story, every month.

Hind Al Oud, a fragrance house in the beauty sector, needed a steady stream of social content with the polish of a campaign: moodboards, editorial stills and lifestyle, refreshed every month.

Each month starts with a moodboard. We pick the mood, feel, environment and direction: beige, gold and sand, the warm, quiet luxury the fragrance world lives in.

From there we gather the source files, clean them up, and put it all together to the brief, a full nine-post grid plus the supporting frames around it.

Seventeen images and four videos come out of each cycle. Not all of it ships at once. The strongest frames are scheduled and published across the month.

Everything is cleaned up and finished in-studio, on palette and on brand, so the feed stays consistent month over month.

05 The Approach

Moodboard. Campaign. Content.

A repeatable monthly pipeline, from palette to a published feed, on brand every cycle.

Phase 01 · Moodboard

Set the Direction

We pick the mood, feel, environment and direction, and assemble it all into a moodboard, beige, gold and sand, before anything is produced.

Phase 02 · Curate

Gather & Clean

We gather the source files the moodboard calls for, then clean them up so everything sits to one consistent standard.

Phase 03 · Produce

To the Brief

We put it all together and produce the content to the brief, a nine-post grid, supporting frames and reels, finished in-studio.
