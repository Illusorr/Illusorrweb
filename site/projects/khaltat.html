01 The Brief

Two streams, one brand.

Khaltat needed both a monthly social presence and clean, product-led e-commerce imagery, two different jobs, produced with AI on one consistent brand.

The social side is a monthly feed: a nine-post grid built to read as one composition, plus supporting frames and short videos around it.

The e-commerce side is product-first: clean, considered shots made to sit on the store and carry the fragrance line.

Every cycle opens with a moodboard that locks the mood, feel, environment and direction, navy, with orange and red accents, before anything is produced.

From there we gather the source files, clean them up, and put it all together to the brief. We publish a selection, not everything we make.

02 Social Media

Six months of feed. One grid at a time.

A rolling social campaign from February to July: every month planned, shot and delivered as a complete nine-post grid, so the feed reads as one composition rather than a run of separate posts.

Fifty-four posts across six monthly drops, plus reels and extra cuts delivered alongside each grid.

03 E-Commerce

The store set. Every product, every angle.

The e-commerce library: each product built as its own set of views, so a listing can carry the bottle, the box and the detail without a second shoot.

05 The Approach

Moodboard. Curate. Produce.

A repeatable workflow that feeds two streams, a monthly social grid and a product-led e-commerce set, from one brand direction.

Phase 01 · Moodboard

Set the Direction

We pick the mood, feel, environment and direction, and assemble it all into a moodboard before anything is produced.

Phase 02 · Curate

Gather & Clean

We gather the source files the moodboard calls for, then clean them up so everything sits to one consistent standard.

Phase 03 · Produce

To the Brief

We put it all together and produce the content to the brief, finished in-studio and ready for social and the store.
