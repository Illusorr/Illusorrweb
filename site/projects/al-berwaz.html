01The Brief

Nobody buys “insurance.”

They buy group health for forty staff, or cover for one car, or a marine cargo policy. A broker’s site fails the moment it makes a facilities manager wade through travel insurance to find liability.

Al Berwaz has broked in Abu Dhabi since 1995, and the catalogue reflects that: thirteen lines spanning financial lines and trade credit at one end, pleasure craft and home at the other, marine and engineering in a category of their own.

So the split came first. Three families, named for who is buying rather than how insurers classify risk, and a quote form that already knows which family you came from, so nobody chooses from thirteen options in a dropdown.

07The Approach

Sort the catalogue. Then design the enquiry.

Three passes, and the form was specified before any page was drawn.

Phase 01 · Sort

Group by Buyer

Thirteen lines were listed and regrouped by who enquires rather than by class of risk. That produced three families, and the three families produced the navigation.

Phase 02 · Funnel

Fork the Form

One quote form became four, each pre-scoped to its family. The generic form stays for anyone arriving from the header with no context yet.

Phase 03 · Build

Hand Over Editable

Built on a managed platform so the team adds lines, carriers and testimony themselves. Thirty years of relationships change faster than a website budget.

A broker that reads as a specialist. Whichever door you came through.
