A digital twin MVP for Bawtaqah Research Center in Riyadh: RiyadhVerse explores the future of urban digital twins, pioneering new methods of visualising and storing data on past and present cities. Built by ILLUSORR.

01 What it is

A living digital twin, built in Unreal Engine.

RiyadhVerse is a digital twin of Riyadh with a live database wired directly to the buildings: select any structure in the city and its real records surface on the spot.

Real time

The city geometry was modelled and lit as a real-time environment, so it can be navigated at street level or read from above, without pre-rendering a single frame.

Connected

Every building carries an identity that maps to a record in the connected database. Update the data and the twin updates with it: the model and the archive stay one thing, not two.
