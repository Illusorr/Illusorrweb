01 The Concept

A world expo, without walls.

Optiverse was envisioned as a digital expo for the future, a metaverse designed exclusively for Optima and built as a monumental virtual hub.

Inspired by the idea of a world expo, Optiverse became a space of endless exploration with no physical limitations, a place audiences could enter to explore, connect and discover.

Rather than a single venue, it was conceived as a living ecosystem of worlds, each tuned to a different kind of gathering, talk or cultural showcase.

The design merged art and architecture into a fluid, multi-level environment, generated through procedural modeling so the space could grow far beyond what conventional construction allows.

Every threshold was treated as a transition between worlds, stitched together by portals, maps and layered pathways into one continuous experience.

02 Procedural Aesthetic

An architecture that questions the rules.

Procedural modeling let us challenge the conventions of physical space. In a world without gravity, the old assumptions stop applying.
