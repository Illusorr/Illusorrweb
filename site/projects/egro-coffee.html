01 The Brief

One brand, one story.

Egro Coffee needed a brand identity that could carry a specialty coffee offer with warmth, clarity and contemporary confidence.

The brief was clear: build a brand from the ground up. Logo, colour, type, packaging, and a design language consistent enough to carry the brand across every surface.

We opened with research — the specialty coffee landscape, the Egro audience, and what it means to offer a premium product in a market crowded with noise.

From research to direction: a visual language built on warmth, restraint and craft. The palette, the mark and the type system all reinforce one idea — quality without noise.

The result is a complete brand identity, production-ready across packaging, collateral and digital, that gives Egro Coffee a distinct voice in the F&B space.

05 The Approach

Research. Design. Deliver.

A three-phase workflow: research sets the direction, design builds the system, and delivery produces the final brand assets ready for production.

Phase 01 · Research

Set the Direction

We study the market, the audience and the brand ambition — then lock a visual direction that everything from the logo to the packaging is built from.

Phase 02 · Design

Build the System

We design the logo, colour palette, typography and usage rules — then apply them across every surface: packaging, collateral, digital and campaign.

Phase 03 · Delivery

Package & Deliver

We produce the full asset set — production-ready files, brand guidelines and campaign visuals — finished to spec and ready for use across every channel.
