01The Brief

One studio. The full stack.

Lapillus needed a digital home as considered as the residence itself. Fast, immersive, and unmistakably its own across every device.

We designed the site for desktop and mobile as one system: a warm stone palette, editorial type, and a layout that lets every space breathe like a gallery.

Interactive storytelling carries the visitor through the residence: spaces reveal on scroll, sequences narrate the design, and the architecture is felt rather than listed.

Real-time 3D brings the residence into the browser. We optimised every scene so it loads fast and holds a smooth frame rate on phones as well as desktops.

AI-driven content and a social system keep the feed alive between releases: on palette, on brand, and produced in-studio.
