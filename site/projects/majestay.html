01 The Brief

A luxury tourism platform. Built for both worlds.

Majestay, a tourism and hospitality brand, needed a refined web presence that could communicate luxury travel experiences to local and international audiences with equal fluency.

Majestay's offer spans luxury homestay accommodation and curated travel experiences: a dual proposition that required a platform flexible enough to showcase both with the right tone for each.

We approached the design through the lens of destination storytelling: before writing a line of code, we mapped what makes a traveller choose Majestay over any alternative. The answer was warmth, specificity and the feeling of being genuinely looked after.

A refined website crafted for Majestay, a tourism and hospitality brand. The platform highlights luxury homestay and travel experiences, balancing modern visuals with a welcoming tone to appeal to both local and international audiences.

The result is an immersive, AI-enhanced web experience that leads with story: destination imagery, editorial content and a seamless front-end that makes every interaction feel considered and unhurried.

02 The Platform

Seven capabilities. One experience.

Every feature of the Majestay platform was designed to reduce friction between a traveller's desire and their decision to book.

01
AI Content

Destination content, property descriptions and travel guides generated and refined with AI: consistent in tone, tailored to the reader, updated in real time.

02
Interaction Design

Scroll-triggered reveals, parallax destination layers and micro-animations that make browsing feel like arriving: an interaction language built for luxury travel.

03
Front End Build

A performant, accessible front-end built to handle rich visual content without compromising on speed: fast load times, smooth transitions, flawless across all devices.

04
Storytelling

Editorial-quality destination narratives and property stories that give each experience a distinct voice: copy, structure and visual hierarchy working together.

05
Brand Identity

A visual identity system designed for a brand that speaks equally to local and international travellers: warm, refined and unmistakably Majestay.

06
Immersive Experience

Immersive web techniques: full-bleed video, ambient sound cues, panoramic image sequences, that place the traveller inside each destination before they leave the page.

03 The Approach

Strategy. Story. Build.

From brand positioning to a live platform: a full-stack creative and development pipeline that treated every design decision as a hospitality decision.

Phase 01 · Brand & Strategy

Position the Brand

We mapped Majestay's offer against the competitive landscape, identified the tone that would resonate with both local and international travellers, and built the visual identity and messaging from that position.

Phase 02 · Design & Content

Design the Experience

Destination storytelling, AI-generated editorial content, immersive visual sequences and an interaction language that makes the platform feel as considered as the properties it represents.

Phase 03 · Build & Launch

Ship the Platform

A performant, responsive front-end built and deployed: designed to grow as Majestay adds new destinations, properties and experiences over time.
